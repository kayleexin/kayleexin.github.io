Music

Wang Yibo is a talented singer and dancer who first became known as a member of the boy group UNIQ. His music mainly focuses on hip-hop and dance styles, showing strong rhythm and energy. He has released popular songs like Fire and No Sense, which highlight his unique style and stage presence. With his excellent dancing skills and cool performance, he has gained many fans in the music industry. In addition, he often performs on stage and in variety shows, where he shows his strong dance ability and confidence. His passion for music and hard work help him continue to grow as an artist and reach a wider audience.
